Project 01 · Organic · 2024

Diels-Alder transition states

Endo / exo selectivity mapped across 12 dienophile partners using B3LYP-D3 with DLPNO-CCSD(T) refinement.

~18,000 CPU-hr · 12 substrates · 24 transition states

§ 1Motivation

The Diels-Alder cycloaddition is a textbook concerted reaction, but endo / exo selectivity is governed by subtle secondary-orbital and steric effects. The goal here was to systematically map both transition states across a series of dienophiles and quantify what drives the preference — not just to predict it.

§ 2Software stack

Electronic structure
Gaussian 16, B3LYP-D3/6-31G(d)
Single-point refinement
DLPNO-CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ in ORCA
Structure building / viz
GaussView, Avogadro
Analysis & plotting
Python (cclib, NumPy, Matplotlib)

§ 3Workflow

For each diene/dienophile pair: optimized reactants and product, located both endo and exo transition states with QST3, confirmed each TS with a single imaginary frequency, ran IRC in both directions to verify connectivity, then refined energies with DLPNO-CCSD(T) single points. Activation strain analysis decomposed the barrier into distortion and interaction terms.

R² strain vs barrier

Endo preference correlated more strongly with interaction energy (secondary orbital overlap) than with steric distortion, except for the two bulky dienophiles where exo dominated. Including D3 dispersion was essential — without it, three substrates flipped preference incorrectly.

§ 6Deliverables

Repository of optimized geometries (.xyz, .log files), Jupyter notebooks for the activation strain analysis, and a poster summarizing the substrate scope. Available on request.
